## Current Projects

### Summer Research Internship (IISER Mohali, 2026)

**Supervisor:** Prof. Amit Kulshrestha

Research topic:

> Computational aspects of alternating tensors, exterior algebra, and the classification of skew-symmetric 3-tensors.

The work includes implementations of algorithms related to

- Exterior algebra operations
- Wedge products
- Contraction maps
- Plücker relations
- Bryant's classification of alternating 3-forms in \(\Lambda^3(\mathbb{R}^6)\)
- Hitchin's invariant and stable forms
- Computational verification of orbit classification
- Randomized computational experiments
- Exploratory computations in \(\Lambda^3(\mathbb{R}^7)\)
